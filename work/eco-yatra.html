Eco-Yatra

A sustainable travel partner that tracks your carbon.

Role
Team member
Stack
ML, data analytics
Result
1st, Sustainable Cities
Year
2024

Eco-Yatra promotes sustainable transport by tracking carbon emissions and suggesting eco-friendly routes with ML and data analytics. Built in a weekend11Held at KIIT University from 19 to 21 September 2024. with Rudra Prasanna Mishra and D Vamsi Krishna, it won first place in the Sustainable Cities theme of the WATCH Hackathon, run by the US Consulates in Hyderabad and Mumbai with CUNY and Bronx Community College.
