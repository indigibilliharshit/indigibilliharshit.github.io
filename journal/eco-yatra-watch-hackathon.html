Eco-Yatra and a weekend at the WATCH Hackathon

First place in the Sustainable Cities theme, 19 to 21 September 2024.

Our team from Sri Sri University won first place in the Sustainable Cities theme at the WATCH (Weather and Climate) Hackathon, held at KIIT University from 19 to 21 September 2024.

Our project, Eco-Yatra, is a sustainable travel partner. It promotes greener transport, tracks carbon emissions, and suggests eco-friendly routes using ML and data analytics.

I built it with Rudra Prasanna Mishra and D Vamsi Krishna. Thank you to the jury, including Peter Groffman, Shalini Bharat, Asutosh Acharya, Aditya Vaishya, Ankit Saxena and Shweta Saxena, and to KIIT, KISS, CUNY Bronx Community College, the City University of New York, and the US Consulates General in Mumbai and Hyderabad for creating the platform.

A special thank you to Achyuta Samanta, founder of KIIT and KISS, who listened to our idea with real interest and offered words of encouragement I still remember.
